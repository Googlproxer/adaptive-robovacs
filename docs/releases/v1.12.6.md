# Adaptive RoboVacs v1.12.6

## User-visible changes

- The Floor plan card fits each floor diagram to the available viewport height.
  On ultrawide displays, a plan remains proportionate and centred instead of
  becoming tall enough to require scrolling through the diagram.
- The editor accounts for the empty horizontal space around a centred diagram,
  so room, marker, and connection interactions stay correctly aligned.

## Migration and safety

- No durable floor-plan, scheduler, occupancy, or cleaning data changes.
